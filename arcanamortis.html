Arcana Mortis

A card-based FPS roguelike made in Unity using C#. I worked as a contract Gameplay Programmer for Misfire Entertainment, creating Melee Weapons, Held Items, various card effects, and a variety of other smaller features. Shown is some of the scripting for melee weapons, and one of them being used in gameplay. This project taught me a lot about working in larger groups on more complex projects.
