About Me

I am a Gameplay Programmer creating interesting mechanics to realise the vision of Game Designers.

As a Gameplay Programmer, I love to see games come together from their original designs with my work. I've made a variety of projects, from games with a primarily narrative focus, to pathfinding simulations, to more complex strategy games, across multiple engines such as Unity and Unreal. I'm aiming to improve my skills by working in a team with other programmers, and to create innovative games together.
